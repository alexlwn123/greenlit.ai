# Product framing: system of record and execution layer

## Decision

Frame Greenlit as the system of record and execution layer for regulatory
submissions. The product helps teams organize evidence, coordinate submission
readiness, prepare a preliminary dossier, and identify gaps before final counsel
review.

## Capability sequence

1. Structured intake and institutional knowledge
2. Submission workflow orchestration
3. Evidence-grounded preliminary drafting
4. Gap analysis and readiness review

## Positioning guardrails

- Do not claim to replace regulatory counsel.
- Do not characterize generated output as final legal advice.
- Emphasize traceability, readiness, and human review.
- Avoid promising a specific reduction in counsel fees until measured.
- Treat the claim that coordination is the primary pain as a hypothesis, not fact.

## Rationale

This sequence begins with a lower-trust workflow, makes drafting downstream of
structured evidence, and better reflects the technical dependencies that govern
submission readiness.
