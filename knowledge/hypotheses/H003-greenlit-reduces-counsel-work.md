# Greenlit materially reduces counsel work

## Claim

A structured evidence package and credible preliminary dossier will materially
reduce outside counsel's assembly and drafting work without reducing necessary
expert review.

## Evidence for

- Ryan found the proposition of completing much of the work before final counsel
  review compelling in principle.
- He observed a broader shift toward bringing counsel a draft and using them for
  editing, judgment, and liability coverage.

## Evidence against or unresolved

- No billing data or task-level baseline has been collected.
- Counsel may need to repeat work to satisfy professional obligations.
- Technical studies and process readiness, rather than legal drafting, may drive
  most cost and elapsed time.

## Falsification criteria

- Counsel cannot rely on the source package and repeats the same assembly work.
- Savings are immaterial relative to total submission cost and risk.
- Review costs increase because generated materials introduce subtle errors.

## Next tests

- Map counsel tasks, hours, rates, and dependencies on two representative matters.
- Ask counsel what characteristics make a client-prepared draft useful or harmful.
- Compare review time for an ordinary package and a Greenlit-structured package.
