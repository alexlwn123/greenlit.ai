# The company is Greenlit's initial customer

## Claim

Food and biotechnology companies preparing regulatory submissions will adopt and
pay for Greenlit before regulatory law firms do.

## Evidence for

- Ryan described a credible company pitch: complete much of the preparation with
  Greenlit, then take a more complete package to counsel.
- Companies face existential regulatory risk and can justify meaningful spend.

## Evidence against or unresolved

- Specialized firms already control the workflow and may be better-positioned buyers.
- No active company has yet committed budget or a pilot for this framing.
- Counsel acceptance may be necessary even if the company pays.

## Falsification criteria

- Companies consistently defer tool selection to counsel.
- Counsel refuses to review or rely on Greenlit-prepared materials.
- Companies value the workflow but will not allocate budget.

## Next tests

- Interview the internal regulatory owner at three active submissions.
- Interview at least two specialist counsel users about acceptable inputs.
- Ask who owns budget, procurement, implementation, and final approval separately.
