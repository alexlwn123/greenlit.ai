## Ideal historical validation partner

- Has retained underlying drafts, study reports, process descriptions, and counsel requests.
- Has a completed dossier that can be withheld during reconstruction.
- Can permit structured review under appropriate confidentiality controls.
- Will allow expert comparison of Greenlit's output against the final work product.

## Ideal active pilot

- Is early enough that Greenlit can observe intake and readiness work.
- Continues its normal counsel-led process in parallel.
- Has an internal owner willing to provide weekly feedback.
- Agrees in advance on evaluation criteria, including completeness, traceability,
  errors, review time, and gaps surfaced.
