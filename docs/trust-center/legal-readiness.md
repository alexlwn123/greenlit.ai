# Legal and commercial readiness

This is an issue checklist, not legal advice or an executed agreement. Qualified counsel must tailor it to Greenlit's entity, customers, jurisdictions, insurance, and actual deployment.

## Master agreement / delivery terms

Define the services and acceptance; permitted users; customer instructions; support and availability; security schedule; confidentiality; customer ownership of inputs; limited license to process inputs; ownership/license for outputs and product IP; AI limitations and required human review; fees; term/termination; export and deletion; warranties/disclaimers; indemnities; liability cap and carve-outs; insurance; audit/evidence process; subcontracting; publicity; governing law; and order of precedence.

Avoid promising regulatory approval, scientific sufficiency, legal advice, error-free AI, absolute security, uninterrupted availability, or controls that the register marks incomplete.

## Data processing addendum

Address controller/processor roles and subject matter; documented instructions; confidentiality; security measures; subprocessor authorization and flow-down; data-subject assistance; breach cooperation/notification; DPIA/regulator assistance; deletion or return; audit information; processing locations; cross-border mechanism; government requests; and conflict/order of precedence. Attach a deployment-specific data/category/purpose/retention/subprocessor schedule.

## Security schedule

Incorporate only evidence-backed controls. Define security contacts, vulnerability handling, access, encryption, logging, SDLC, incident response, continuity, deletion, AI route, customer responsibilities, and notification method. State whether customer gateway responsibilities are shared or customer-controlled.

## Regulated data

- **HIPAA:** do not accept PHI or claim compliance until applicability is confirmed, BAAs exist with the customer and every relevant vendor, a documented risk analysis is completed, and administrative/physical/technical safeguards operate.
- **Clinical/GxP/Part 11:** Greenlit is not represented as a validated electronic-record/signature system. Define intended use and validation responsibility before regulated records rely on it.
- **Export-controlled or classified data:** prohibit unless a specifically approved deployment, personnel, region, and contract support it.

## Corporate evidence typically requested

Certificate of incorporation/good standing; tax and banking forms; ownership/conflict disclosures; cyber and technology E&O certificates; sanctions/anti-bribery commitments; workforce confidentiality and invention assignment; background-screening policy where lawful; security/privacy training records; approved policies; SOC/penetration evidence; and business references. Store sensitive corporate evidence in a controlled diligence room, not this public repository.
