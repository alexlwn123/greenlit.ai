# SOC 2 vendor-selection strategy

## Recommendation

Collect three independent CPA quotes and three compliance-platform quotes, plus at least two penetration-test quotes. Select the CPA on report quality, independence, scope judgment, secure evidence handling, predictable requests, and a clean transition to Type II—not simply the shortest advertised timeline.

For Greenlit's current size, compare a manual-first track using the existing repository evidence against Vanta, Drata, and Secureframe. A platform is worthwhile only if its integrations reduce real evidence labor and it exports all evidence cleanly. It is not a substitute for control operation or the independent CPA report.

## Current market guardrails

Most large platforms use customized pricing. Public 2026 marketplace comparisons show materially different scopes and employee bands, so headline prices should not be compared without a written bill of materials. Require price protection and separately identify framework, trust-center, vendor-risk, implementation, auditor, and renewal charges.

Do not accept an auditor solely because a platform recommends it. The AICPA has specifically highlighted ethics risks where SOC tool providers and CPA firms have business relationships. Require written independence and referral-fee disclosure.

## Proposed shortlist for quotes

### Compliance workflow

- Manual-first using Greenlit's current JSON control/risk/remediation registers and a restricted evidence room.
- Vanta Essentials or equivalent one-framework package.
- Secureframe one-framework package.
- Drata one-framework package.

### CPA auditor

Request firms through at least two channels: direct startup-focused CPA firms and the selected platform's auditor directory. Before shortlisting, verify the firm and signing CPA through the relevant state board, request peer-review information, references, a sample request list, and disclosure of platform relationships. Do not treat a comparison-directory “verified” badge as sufficient.

### Penetration test

Prefer a focused, authenticated SaaS web/API assessment with manual authorization and business-logic testing. A low-cost scanner report does not adequately test Greenlit's tenant, capability-link, upload, and model-routing risks. Require an included retest and an auditor/customer-facing executive letter.

## Decision gates

1. Alex confirms whether the partner accepts Type I and whether Confidentiality/Availability are required.
2. Management confirms entity, owners, workforce/access population, deadline, and maximum first-year/renewal budget.
3. Auditor reviews the proposed boundary and control matrix before the as-of date is selected.
4. Platform is purchased only after a proof-of-concept demonstrates integrations and evidence export.
5. Penetration scope is approved by the SOC auditor before testing so the resulting report satisfies evidence expectations.

## Non-negotiables

- Independent licensed CPA firm issues the report.
- No guaranteed “pass,” backdated control evidence, or fabricated historical operation.
- No production customer content in sales demos or generic support tickets.
- NDA and secure evidence room before restricted evidence transfer.
- Fixed, documented scope and pricing with Type II continuation.
- Full evidence and control export on termination.
- No use of Greenlit evidence to train shared AI models.
